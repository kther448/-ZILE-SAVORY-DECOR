Our Story

Haitian roots. Limitless creativity.

Meet the passion behind Zile Savory & Decor and the woman bringing it to life.

Isabelle Jerome, Founder

A passion turned purpose.

Guided by determination and a love of food, Isabelle Jerome founded Zile Savory & Decor to transform nostalgia for Haitian flavors into something warm, welcoming, and accessible.

For Isabelle, cooking is more than a profession. It is a language that connects generations, places, and memories. Her dishes keep Haitian culture alive around the table.

That creative spirit extends to event décor — styling birthdays, brunches, and special gatherings with color, care, and personality.

Currently studying culinary arts, Isabelle continues to sharpen her craft. Her bartending certificate adds another dimension to her hospitality and presentation.

“I want every guest to feel at home, even when home is far away.”
